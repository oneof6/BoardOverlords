📖 How to Play Solo

1. Create a character with your favorite RPG's rules. 2. Roll a Plot Hook and set the opening scene. 3. Ask the Oracle yes/no or how-much questions whenever the story needs an answer only a GM would know. Interpret every result in context — the answer should feel logical, even when it's surprising. 4. Play out the scene using your game's normal rules for challenges and combat (use the Battle tab for foes). 5. Use GM Moves to keep the action moving between checks or after a failure. 6. When the scene ends, adjust the Chaos Factor: raise it after a wild, chaotic scene; lower it after a calm one that went as expected. 7. Set the next scene and continue.

🔮 Reading the Oracle

Choose the odds that feel right for your question, then roll. Chaos shifts the odds by 5% per point away from the middle (Chaos 5): higher chaos favors "Yes" and more surprises; lower chaos favors calm, expected outcomes. A roll deep in the top or bottom fifth of the range is an Exceptional result — take it further than a plain yes or no. On any double-digit roll (11, 22, 33 … 99) where the repeated digit is at or below the current Chaos Factor, a Random Event has occurred — roll one on the Random Event tool.

⚔️ Running the Enemy AI

Add each foe to the roster with its starting health. As the fight goes on, adjust health and morale. When it's the enemy's turn and you don't know what it would do, roll Enemy Intent — it reads the creature's condition and hands you a believable action: an unhurt, confident brute fights hard, while a bloodied, wavering coward looks for the exit. Use Target Selection to decide who it goes after, and Morale Check whenever a foe first drops below half health or watches an ally fall.
